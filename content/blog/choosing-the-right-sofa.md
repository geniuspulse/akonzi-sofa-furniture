# How to Choose the Right Sofa for Your Living Room

Choosing the right sofa is one of the most important furniture decisions you'll make. It's the centerpiece of your living room — where you relax, entertain, and spend time with family. Here's our guide to making the right choice.

## 1. Measure Your Space

Before anything else, measure your living room. You need to know:
- The length and width of the area where the sofa will go
- The width of doorways and hallways (the sofa needs to fit through!)
- Leave at least 18 inches of space around the sofa for walking

## 2. Choose Your Style

- **3-Seater Sofa**: Perfect for smaller living rooms or as an addition to a larger set
- **3+2+ Seater Set**: A complete set with a 3-seater and 2-seater — great for family rooms
- **3+1+1 Seater Set**: A versatile set with a 3-seater and two single chairs

## 3. Consider the Material

The fabric or material you choose affects both comfort and durability:
- **Leather**: Durable, easy to clean, ages beautifully
- **Fabric**: Softer, more color options, cozy feel
- **Mixed**: Best of both worlds — leather on high-wear areas, fabric on seating

## 4. Think About Color

Choose a color that complements your walls and decor. Neutral tones (brown, beige, grey) are versatile and timeless. Bold colors make a statement but can be harder to match.
