# Welcome to Akonzi Sofa Furniture

At Akonzi Sofa Furniture, we believe that great furniture should be accessible to everyone. Based in Lilongwe, Malawi, we've been crafting quality sofas, dining sets, and beds for homes across the country.

## Our Story

What started as a small workshop has grown into a trusted furniture brand in Lilongwe. We make and supply quality, affordable, and durable furniture — each piece made to order and built to last.

## Why Choose Akonzi?

- **Quality Craftsmanship**: Every piece is handmade by skilled artisans
- **Affordable Prices**: Quality furniture that fits your budget
- **Durable Materials**: Built to last for years, not seasons
- **Free Delivery**: We deliver for free within Lilongwe

## Browse Our Collection

From sofa sets to dining tables, coffee tables to bed frames — we have something for every room in your home. Each piece can be customized to your preferences. Simply message us on WhatsApp to place an order.
